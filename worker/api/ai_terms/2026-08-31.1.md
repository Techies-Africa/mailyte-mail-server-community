# Mailyte AI Assistant (Maya) - Terms of Use and Consent

Version: 2026-08-31.1
Effective: 31 August 2026

These terms describe what happens when you turn on Maya, the AI assistant
built into your Mailyte mailbox. Please read them before you accept. You are
agreeing to THIS version only. If we change these terms we will publish a new
version and ask you again; your acceptance of one version is never treated as
acceptance of a later one.

## 1. What Maya does

When you ask it to, Maya can:

- draft or rewrite a message from your instruction;
- summarise a single message or a whole conversation;
- suggest a label (for example "newsletter" or "action required") for
  messages in your inbox.

Maya only acts when you invoke it. It does not read your mailbox in the
background, and it does not send mail on its own.

## 2. What is sent to the AI provider

To answer a request, Mailyte sends the relevant text to the AI model
configured for this deployment. Depending on the feature that can include:

- the instruction you typed and any draft you asked Maya to revise;
- the subject, sender, date and body text of the message or conversation you
  asked Maya to summarise;
- the subject and a short preview of each message you asked Maya to label.

The AI model may be operated by a third-party provider chosen by the operator
of this Mailyte deployment. Text sent to the model leaves Mailyte's own
systems for the duration of the request. Attachments are never sent.

## 3. Redaction is best-effort, not guaranteed

Before text is sent, Mailyte applies an automatic filter that tries to mask
obvious payment card numbers and similarly formatted identifiers. This filter
is best-effort only. It will miss things, and it cannot recognise every kind
of sensitive information. You should assume that anything in a message you
ask Maya to process may reach the AI provider unmasked. Do not use Maya on
messages you would not be comfortable sharing with an external service.

## 4. Your mailbox contains other people's personal data

The messages in your mailbox were written by, addressed to and mention other
people: colleagues, customers, friends, family. When you ask Maya to process a
message you are also sending those people's personal data to the AI provider.
You are responsible for using Maya only where that is appropriate and lawful
in your circumstances, and in line with any rules your organisation sets.

## 5. Two separate decisions

You make two independent choices:

1. Use of the assistant. This lets Maya process the text you send it, for the
   sole purpose of answering that request. Text is not kept by Mailyte for AI
   purposes after the response is produced.

2. Use of your mail to improve AI models ("training"). This is optional and
   is OFF unless you turn it on. If you accept it, requests you send to Maya,
   and the responses, may be retained by Mailyte or its AI provider and used
   to evaluate and improve the assistant. Declining training does not limit
   any Maya feature.

You can withdraw either decision at any time from your mailbox settings.
Withdrawing training stops future retention; it does not recall data already
used.

## 6. Your organisation's controls

If your mailbox belongs to an organisation, its administrators decide whether
Maya is available at all. They can block it for everyone, in which case your
personal acceptance no longer has any effect, or accept these terms on behalf
of the whole organisation, in which case you will not be asked individually.
Either way, Maya also requires that this deployment has an AI provider
configured and that your organisation's plan includes it.

## 7. Limits and accuracy

Each mailbox has a monthly allowance of Maya requests, set by your
organisation's plan. Maya can be wrong: summaries may omit or misstate
things, drafts may say what you did not mean, and labels may be mistaken.
Read what it produces before you rely on it or send it.

## 8. Your record of this consent

When you accept, Mailyte creates a record of exactly what you agreed to: your
mailbox address, both decisions, this version of the terms and its full text,
the time, and who recorded it. The record is stored with a cryptographic
integrity digest so it can be shown later that it has not been altered, and
you can download it as a PDF at any time from your consent history. The same
record is kept when you withdraw.

## 9. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms we will publish a new version and Maya will be
paused for you until you have read and accepted it. Consent to one version is
not consent to the next.

## 10. Contact

Questions about these terms go to the operator of this Mailyte deployment or,
for mailboxes hosted by Mailyte, to privacy@mailyte.com.
